Where Time Gets Lost

Work That Adds Up
Across Every Week

In most manufacturing shops, a meaningful portion of the workweek goes to tasks that are manual and repetitive. These are the areas where the right automation tends to make the most difference.

  • 01

    Quoting done by hand

    Pulling material costs, calculating labor, and formatting estimates manually takes significant time, and the process often lives with one person.

  • 02

    Reports compiled on demand

    When visibility into operations requires someone to stop and pull data from multiple places, it either doesn't happen consistently or takes up time it shouldn't.

  • 03

    Onboarding that depends on who's available

    Without structured materials, new hire training varies based on who's doing it that week, and your experienced people absorb the cost of that inconsistency.

  • 04

    Process knowledge that isn't written down

    Procedures, workarounds, and institutional knowledge stored in experienced workers' heads aren't transferable and create real gaps when those people are unavailable.

  • 05

    Documentation that falls out of date

    SOPs and work instructions that are difficult to update stop getting used. Teams end up reverting to informal methods, and the documentation becomes a formality.

What We Build

Five Tools for
Manufacturing Operations

Each tool we build targets a specific, recurring time drain. We scope it to your operation, build it to fit how your team works, and make sure it's something your people will actually use.

01

Automated Quote Generator

Estimating

A system that generates accurate quotes using your existing pricing, materials, and labor data. It connects to how your shop already calculates jobs and outputs estimates in a consistent format, reducing the time required per quote and keeping the process from depending on one person's spreadsheet.

02

Automated Reporting

Operations

Scheduled reports built around the metrics your operation tracks: production output, job status, cost by job, or whatever your team actually needs to see. Data is pulled and formatted automatically, delivered on a schedule, without requiring anyone to compile it by hand.

03

Sales Prep Workflow

Sales

Before a call with a prospect, your sales team gets a structured briefing: company background, likely pain points based on their industry and size, and specific areas where your products or services could add value. It turns a cold call into a prepared conversation and cuts the time it takes to get ready for a meeting.

04

Hiring & Onboarding Materials

People

Structured materials that standardize how your shop hires and brings people on: job descriptions, interview guides, training checklists, and step-by-step onboarding flows. Reduces the variation in how onboarding gets done and takes the burden off your experienced staff to recreate the process every time.

05

Process Knowledge Tools

Knowledge

Software that answers questions about how work gets done in your shop, based on your own documented processes. A new hire or a worker on a different shift can type in a question and get an answer pulled directly from your SOPs and work instructions, without having to find someone to ask.
