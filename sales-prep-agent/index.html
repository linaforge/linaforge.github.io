The example company: Ironwood Robotics

To show you this tool we had to build it for someone, so we invented a company. Ironwood Robotics is a fictional regional dealer of commercial service robots. They sell and lease four families of robot to businesses that have large floors, long distances, and staff who spend their shift walking: hotels, hospitals, warehouses, campuses, and the like. They are not a manufacturer. They are a dealer with a catalog, a price list, and a sales team making cold calls.

That last part matters. The agent has been loaded with Ironwood's entire commercial position — what each robot does, what it costs, what the lease terms are, what the service guarantee is. That is why the briefs below can do labor math and answer objections with real numbers instead of describing a company back to you. Any research tool can summarize a prospect's website. This one arrives quote-ready because it knows what the rep is actually selling.

If we built this for your business, everything in the panel below would be replaced with your products, your pricing, your terms, and the objections your reps actually hear.
