What you just watched

A bid package like the ones above takes a sales engineer two to four hours to read properly, and most shops do not have those hours. So packages get skimmed, the wrong ones get bid, and the disqualifying detail turns up after the quote goes out. This agent reads the whole thing in under a minute and puts the disqualifying detail on the first screen.

  • It reads your actual documentsPDFs, Word files, spreadsheets, zipped bid packages. Scanned drawings and multi-column specs included.
  • It knows what you can buildYour certifications, catalog limits, and commercial terms are loaded in, so it flags what you fail, not just what they asked.
  • It tells you when to walkThe third sample is a no-bid. Knowing that on day one is worth more than a polished quote on day fourteen.
  • It is yours, not a subscriptionBuilt around your product line, your ten questions, your quoting rules. It runs where you want it to run.
